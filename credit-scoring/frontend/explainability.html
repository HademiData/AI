How to interpret these charts

  • Age distribution: compares the age profiles of applicants with and without recorded difficulties. Bar heights show each outcome group's own distribution, not their population sizes.
  • Age-group rate: shows the percentage with TARGET = 1 in each age group, allowing fairer comparisons between groups of different sizes.
  • Income distribution: shows the shape of reported income within each outcome group, excluding the top 1% of positive values.
  • Credit-to-income ratio: compares requested credit with reported income. A higher ratio means the requested credit is larger relative to reported income.

These charts describe historical data, not causes of payment difficulties. TARGET = 1 represents recorded payment difficulties in this dataset, not certainty about future default.

CreditView · Historical patterns do not establish causation or determine lending decisions.
